# Craft checklist for video review

Apply the [selected editing profile](EDITING_PREFERENCES.md) before choosing footage. Give the reviewer this checklist and the current creative brief.

| Area | Inspect in the render |
| --- | --- |
| Story | The first beat earns attention, the viewer can follow the progression, and the ending resolves the opening’s promise. Factual labels and spoken-picture pairings have evidence. |
| Shot selection | People and action are clear, the complete gestures register, repeats have been removed, and the crop protects the faces, hands and view. Check sharpness and expressions against full-resolution photographs. |
| Pacing | Build around real music accents or speech, with enough time for readable labels and complete actions. Check holds, releases, silence, transitions, scenic payoffs and the tail in motion. |
| Motion design | Use purposeful lettering, leaders and effects. Check attachment and subject clearance through the move, especially on rotating or swinging photographs. Compare entrance, hold and exit on actual footage. |
| Transitions | Use matched action, straight cuts or an authored transition where it earns its place. Inspect both adjacent shots, the exact cut frame and each transparent endpoint. |
| Composition and legibility | Check output-sized captions, font shaping, hierarchy and local contrast. Keep photo layers sharp and clear faces, hands, landmarks, captions and the destination interface throughout motion. |
| Ending | Leave room for the last gesture, view or thought. Check that picture, voice and the authored audio fade end intentionally. |

Inspect the actual movie in its delivery geometry. Record sampled-frame coverage, continuous playback and listening separately. The approved creative reference is evidence for the craft treatment. The [independent review](docs/DIRECTOR_WORKFLOW.md) scores the current movie on its own evidence.
