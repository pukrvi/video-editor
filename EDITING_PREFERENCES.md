# Editing profiles

Apply the matching profile to the first cut. The latest explicit request governs that edit. Keep clip-specific wording, timing and choices in its local brief. Record additional perspectives in local project notes when supplied.

## Narrated vlog

Start story-led vertical vlogs with the accepted Kedarkantha treatment. Duration follows the story. Build from the people, effort, small details, complete comic actions and earned scenic or emotional payoffs. Select the best available material before asking for precise VO pickups. Keep native dialogue, recorded VO and relevant B-roll. Show the named subject when a concrete spoken cue lands. Preserve the speaker’s own words and uncertainty. Verify factual labels and preserve the distinction between testimony and research.

- Open on an expressive person or action. Reveal the scene with a bold, naturally proportioned title that can sit behind a real foreground element or authored matte. Speed up connective B-roll and protect speech, expressions, action endpoints and worthwhile views.
- Use Impact at its natural proportions for large display titles, Futura Condensed Extra Bold for tall chapter entrances and Bradley Hand Bold for cream pencil lettering. Anchor leaders to their subjects and keep the junctions joined during motion. Use a contour peel and subtle air whoosh on a chapter change when the footage earns it. Keep scene graphics and information cards clear of the moving subject, captions and other graphics.
- Use short groups of two or three distinct photographs with staggered arrivals, small independent motion and anchored accents on selected prints. Leave canvas accents in clear space. Preserve faces and hands through entry, hold and exit.
- Keep short, sentence-aware Avenir Next Demi Bold and Devanagari Sangam MN captions distinct from information cards in shape and color. Use a warm active word, clear the highlight during pauses, and keep names and units together within two readable lines. Use alternate placement where a face or action occupies the normal zone. Check the destination’s interface-safe area.
- Use a quiet, smooth folk or similarly appropriate bed under native speech and VO when music fits. Give chapters intentional track handoffs and use sparse interaction or transition effects. Protect intelligibility, verify the isolated bed under both speech roles, and listen to the encoded mix where available. A brief may call for speech-free music instead.
- Check the story for semantic repeats across video and photos. Allow one deliberate opener/body callback when it pays off the opening. Adjacent, nonrepeating windows can form one complete action.

## Music reel

Start from the playful Krabi treatment, with the action and pacing lessons from Phuket. Use a vertical, roughly one-minute cut unless the brief specifies another length. Favor expressive people, distinctive details and progression to an earned view or reaction. Show each moment once. Look for sharp, natural expressions and keep the complete gesture, reveal or action visible at the chosen speed.

- Lead with Futura Bold filled and outlined display lettering, aligned to the shot. Place the optional second line at the lower right and use Bradley Hand Bold for cream pencil scene labels. Draw sparse strokes clear of faces and hands. Join scenic leaders to their label underline and track the real ridge or point through the same picture transform. Test local contrast over pale backgrounds.
- Build a beat map from the actual excerpt. Time live-to-hold, release or cut-away to its accents. Extend the window when a key gesture reads too fast. Use sharp originals for photographs and make each image earn its space. Stagger overlapping prints and add subtle independent swing around one to two degrees when there is space for it. Put contextual accents in gaps or attach them to the print they annotate.
- Use motivated transitions at meaningful changes. Keep the chosen track in the foreground and source audio muted. Start with a two-second tail fade. A compact scenic payoff works when the footage earns it, and a strong final view can finish clear of text.
- Keep text natural and sparse. Use single-line location labels for meaningful stops with sufficient hold time. Place text in available clear space, and verify place names against metadata and visual context.

## Commercial YouTube video

Start with the channel’s current brief and supplied brand kit. Default to landscape 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps. Use a clear opening that earns the title and thumbnail promise, a progression of evidence or story beats, and an ending that serves the requested next action. Choose length, voice, typography, motion and music from the format and brand. When the brand is unspecified, use clean, restrained captions and leave brand decisions in the brief.

Pair demonstrations and factual claims with visible or attributable evidence. Keep an asset list with usage scope and credits for music, fonts, footage, images and graphics. Record applicable sponsorship language, on-screen timing and publication disclosures in the brief. Keep sponsor obligations, title and thumbnail decisions, and delivery requirements tied to the current episode.
