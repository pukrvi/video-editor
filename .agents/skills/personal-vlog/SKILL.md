---
name: personal-vlog
description: Edit a personal narrated travel vlog with native dialogue, recorded voiceover, bilingual captions and story-led B-roll in the approved vertical style.
---

Apply the [narrated-vlog profile](../../../EDITING_PREFERENCES.md#narrated-vlog) on the first cut. Select actual story and speech evidence, align the word clock, and map spoken picture cues before composing. Keep the approved highlighted caption style and a quiet bed when music fits. Ask for focused pickup lines once the available footage shows the gaps.

Start with `./video-editor personal-vlog init --album PATH --slug ID --name TITLE`. Use the [narrated recipe guide](../../../docs/VLOG_WORKFLOW.md) for selected transcription, frame timing and layer authoring. Run `personal-vlog render` and `personal-vlog qa` on that recipe. Inspect the same movie for cue matches, caption shaping and music beneath both speech roles, then follow the [independent review handoff](../../../docs/DIRECTOR_WORKFLOW.md).
