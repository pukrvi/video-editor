---
name: commercial-youtube
description: Edit a commercial YouTube episode from a channel brief, including brand assets, on-screen evidence, captions, asset rights and applicable sponsor deliverables.
---

Apply the [commercial profile](../../../EDITING_PREFERENCES.md#commercial-youtube-video). Start with the episode promise, audience and supplied brand references. Resolve opening evidence, asset usage rights and any sponsor deliverables in the brief before locking the cut. Use the channel brief to choose visual and sound treatments.

Start with `./video-editor commercial-youtube init --album PATH --slug ID --name TITLE`. Use the shared [narrated recipe guide](../../../docs/VLOG_WORKFLOW.md) to place speech, pictures and captions in the landscape starter. Run `commercial-youtube render` and `commercial-youtube qa` on that recipe, then follow the [independent review handoff](../../../docs/DIRECTOR_WORKFLOW.md). Verify any thumbnail, credits or disclosure deliverables against the episode brief.
