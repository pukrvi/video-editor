# Video editing handoff

Match the brief to [personal music reels](.agents/skills/personal-reel/SKILL.md), [personal narrated vlogs](.agents/skills/personal-vlog/SKILL.md) or [commercial YouTube videos](.agents/skills/commercial-youtube/SKILL.md). The [editing profiles](EDITING_PREFERENCES.md) define established defaults. The [craft checklist](REEL_QUALITY_BASELINE.md), [commands](README.md) and [technical lessons](PIPELINE_LEARNINGS.md) apply across workflows.

1. **Scope, style and facts.** Record the exact request, delivery stage, workflow, story and director effort in the local brief. Apply established style choices immediately. The latest explicit direction for this edit takes precedence. Compare short samples when a new direction or material ambiguity warrants a choice. Keep testimony, source observations and researched facts distinct.
2. **Select and align.** Preserve originals, inspect full selected windows for privacy and complete actions, and record the ordered cut with matching source identities. Align the chosen dry native speech and VO takes to the output clock and map concrete spoken cues to the visible subject. For a music reel, establish track accents and action endpoints before adding motion.
3. **Refine in short samples.** Use the normal renderer for the opening, captions, music handoff, transition and photo overlap. Check native-size text, subject clearance, settled/transition frames and the mix. Aim for a resolved first full cut, consolidate corrections into a second, and use a third for finishing if needed. Honor the requested scope and stop point.
4. **Review the exact movie and deliver.** Run technical QA, inspect output and automatically invoke the [independent director](docs/DIRECTOR_WORKFLOW.md) for each draft or revision. Bind evidence to the movie hash and render request. Record actual score, playback/listening limits, quality gate and user choice. Deliver the requested version, offline player and applicable caption sidecars.
5. **Optional timeline and publication.** Preserve reconstruction inputs, credits and sidecars. Follow the [Final Cut policy](docs/FINAL_CUT_EXPORT_POLICY.md) before generating XML or checking the app. Posting footage requires its own authorization.

Keep private conversations and intimate moments out of public edits. Preserve the original sources, local exclusions and earlier versions. Keep precise coordinates, transcripts, media, screening evidence, personal notes, additional persona profiles and license codes in the local workspace. Promote accepted preferences and reproducible fixes to the shared guides.
